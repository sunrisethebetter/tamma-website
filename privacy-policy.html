Privacy Policy.

Your play. Your privacy. Here’s how information is handled across our website, games, and player support.

01About this policy

Tamma (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a casual game studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit tammannn.xyz, play a Tamma game that links to this policy, or contact us. The information processed depends on the particular game, its features, and your choices.

Additional notices in a game or its store listing may describe product-specific practices. Please read those notices alongside this policy. You can contact us at tammaentreprises@gmail.com.

02Information we handle

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any game details, screenshots, or other information you choose to include. Please avoid sending passwords or payment card details.
  • Game and device information. Depending on the game, information may include progress, scores, purchases or entitlements, app interactions, device type, operating system, app version, language, IP address, and crash or performance reports.
  • Advertising information. Games that contain ads may allow advertising providers to process advertising identifiers, ad views, interactions, and approximate location inferred from an IP address. Personalized advertising depends on the relevant consent requirements and your available settings.
  • Permissions. If a game needs a device permission for an optional feature, its purpose will be explained when requested. You can review permissions in your device settings.

03Why information is used

Where applicable, information is used to deliver game features, maintain or restore supported progress, validate purchases, answer player questions, diagnose technical issues, improve gameplay and performance, measure how features are used, provide ads, and prevent cheating, fraud, or misuse.

When applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be based on providing a requested service, our legitimate interests in operating and improving it, a legal obligation, or your consent. We seek consent when required; you may withdraw it through available controls or by contacting us.

04Providers and information sharing

A game may use third-party services for app distribution, payment processing, advertising, analytics, diagnostics, or other supported features. Providers receive the information needed for the services they perform, subject to their roles and applicable rules. Game-specific disclosures and consent screens identify relevant services where required.

App stores generally process payment details under their own terms and privacy policies. We may receive transaction or entitlement information to deliver an item or subscription, rather than your full payment card information.

Information may also be disclosed to comply with lawful requests, protect users and legal rights, investigate security incidents, or in connection with a business transfer with appropriate protections. Providers may operate in countries other than yours; legally required safeguards apply to international transfers.

05Your privacy choices

Your options may include changing advertising and tracking preferences, managing app permissions, declining optional processing, or withdrawing consent through a game, your device settings, or the relevant provider. Restricting a permission may affect a feature that depends on it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Where relevant law provides an opt-out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, you may exercise it through the available privacy controls or by contacting us. We do not sell personal information for money; advertising-related processing may fall within broader legal definitions in some locations.

Send requests to tammaentreprises@gmail.com and identify the game concerned. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity and locate relevant records. Do not send your password. Legal exceptions may limit some requests. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

06Children and age requirements

Check the age rating and eligibility information for each game. Our services are not intended to collect personal information knowingly from children under 13, or under a higher age where local law requires parental consent, without the consent required by law.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information improperly, email us with the game name and relevant details. We will review the concern and remove information or take other appropriate steps where required.

07Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including support, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. The period depends on the type of information and the service involved. Data may then be deleted or de-identified, subject to lawful retention requirements.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. Some gameplay information may remain locally on your device; uninstalling an app may remove local data without deleting records held by a service provider.

08This website and external links

This website has no account registration, contact form, advertising scripts, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by Tamma. It does not request access to your device permissions. Its fonts and illustrations are delivered with the site.

GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub and network providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Clicking an email link opens your email application. Sending an email shares your address and message with us and the email services involved. Other websites and app stores have their own privacy policies.

09Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as our services or information practices change. The effective date above indicates this version. We will provide additional notice or seek consent for changes where applicable law requires it.

For privacy questions, rights requests, or concerns about a game, contact tammaentreprises@gmail.com.
